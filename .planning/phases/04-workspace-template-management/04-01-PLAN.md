<objective>
Deliver the backend slice that unblocks D-06 ("Show deactivated") and D-07 ("Reactivate") on the frontend. The existing list endpoint hard-filters `is_active=True` and `_get_writable_template` 404s inactive rows, so deactivated custom templates can be neither listed nor reactivated today (RESEARCH Pitfall 1). This plan adds an opt-in `include_inactive` list param (D-14) and a dedicated admin-only `reactivate` action (D-15), with contract tests covering listing, reactivation, permission enforcement, and built-in/foreign rejection.

Purpose: Without this, the management UI's Show-deactivated toggle would render an empty list and the Reactivate button would 404. This is the single hard backend dependency of the phase and runs in parallel (Wave 1) with the frontend list.
Output: `include_inactive`-aware `get_queryset`, a `reactivate` viewset action, its URL route, and contract tests.
</objective>
