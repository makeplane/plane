# Video Brief

## Core Promise

See your whole team's work — time, tasks, and people — moving together in one calm, futuristic space.

## Key Points

- Work and working time made **visible** at a glance.
- Tasks **coordinate** themselves across the team (board flow).
- People feel **closer** — presence and connection, not isolation.

## Angle

Not a feature dump. A 10-second "calm reveal": glass UI panels float in, breathe for a beat each, then resolve back into the brand. Minimal text (1–3 words max) to kill typo risk and let motion carry meaning.

---

# Creative Direction

## Art Direction: Modern Tech Futurism

**Core Keywords:** glassmorphism, frosted panels, soft volumetric glow, floating UI, depth-of-field bokeh, premium minimal, blue accent light
**Color Palette:** deep navy-to-charcoal gradient background, frosted translucent white panels, electric Shinhan-blue accent glow, subtle cyan rim light, warm white text
**Signature Effects:** parallax float, slow light sweeps, particle dust, ring/progress fills, light-trail connections between nodes

## Visual Style

A dark, premium gradient void where frosted-glass UI panels drift in with gentle parallax. Each panel is a simplified, idealized version of the real Plane/Workspace UI — a time-progress ring, a Kanban board, a network of avatar nodes. Everything moves **slowly and deliberately** so the eye can read each idea before the next. Soft blue glow is the only saturated color.

## Audio Style

**Music:** Cinematic ambient-tech. Soft synth pad swell + single clean piano/mallet motif, light rising arpeggio into the logo. 70–80 BPM feel, gentle build, warm resolve. No drop, no vocals.
**Voiceover:** None.
**SFX:** Minimal, tactile UI sounds — soft whooshes on panel entry, a light "shimmer" on the avatar connection, a clean chime on the final logo lock.

---

# Characters

None. UI + abstract avatar nodes only (circles, presence dots, cursors). No human faces → no character-consistency work, no facial/typo risk.

# Strategic Evaluation

## Pros

- **Zero typo risk:** captions are ≤3 words and optional; meaning carried by motion. Matches your explicit concern.
- **Slow, legible beats:** 5 scenes over 10s (~2s each) lets viewers "visualize the process" as requested.
- **No faces, no VO:** fast/cheap to produce, fully localizable, no character-consistency or lip-sync work.
- **Strong brand bookend:** opens and closes on Shinhan logo + Shinhan Workspace exactly as specified.

## Cons

- **10s is tight for 3 feature ideas** — each beat is brief; risk of feeling like a teaser rather than an explainer.
- **Abstract UI** communicates "feeling" more than literal product capability; viewers won't see the real app.

## Recommendations

1. **Keep a 20–30s cut in reserve.** Same beats, slower — better for a website hero where the literal product matters.
2. **Optionally swap one abstract panel for a real (lightly stylized) Workspace screenshot** to ground it in the actual product — at the cost of a little typo/realism risk.
3. **Render captions as motion graphics in post**, not baked into AI frames, so text stays crisp and editable per language.

## Why It Should Work

The 10-second window is dominated by a single emotional arc — scattered → visible → in sync → connected → branded — which is exactly the value story of a coordination tool. Modern Tech Futurism reads as "premium, trustworthy, modern," aligning with a banking brand, while glassmorphism keeps it calm rather than flashy. Because comprehension rides on motion and glow (not text), the piece survives sound-off autoplay and cross-language audiences, and the logo bookend maximizes brand recall in a short attention span.
